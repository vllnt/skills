## Principles

- Before work, define outcomes and required proof from the request, instructions, principles, and owning workflow. Keep one owner and validation contract.
- Prove work with runnable candidate-bound checks; add the smallest missing check. Scale independence to risk. Low: producer proof, required checks; medium: add a non-producer rerun and advisory review; high: add targeted review. Only reproduced, independently confirmed, or explicit-requirement findings block. Cap repair rounds; without agents, report missing independence.
- Resolve supported findings within authority; renew affected verdicts. The caller owns changes; verifiers rerun and inspect. Planning improves the plan, assessment the report.
- Synchronize decisions; reuse only current evidence. Settle disagreements with proof, not votes; never lower criteria for acceptance.
- Judge progress by advancing criteria or resolving uncertainty, not activity. When repairs grow or repeat, change approach unprompted or report the unmet criterion, options, and recommendation. Finish when required proof and acceptance hold.
