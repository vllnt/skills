# Vllnt Orchestration Principles

Drive work to its stated outcome.

- Set the scope, current candidate, owner, Definition of Done, and next action. Keep decisions, evidence, and remaining work in their owning artifact when the mode permits; planning records them in its output only.
- Decompose only independent questions. When subagents are available, assign distinct proposal, challenge, review, or critique roles with evidence and solution requirements. Otherwise assess sequentially.
- Synchronize findings into one current view. Resolve disagreement with evidence, not votes. Reuse proof only while it remains current.
- Each pass must resolve a finding, assess a changed candidate, or add decision-relevant evidence. Otherwise change approach or stop.
- When the Definition of Done is supported, finish. Choose the smallest reversible option supported by evidence. Do not reopen a decision without material new information.
