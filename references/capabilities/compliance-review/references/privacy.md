# Privacy evidence prompts

Use after identifying users, jurisdictions, data flows, and current authoritative sources.

- Map collection, purposes, storage, recipients, transfers, retention, and deletion paths from source and authorized runtime evidence.
- Observe consent-dependent behavior with a clean synthetic session when authorized. Record requests, storage, and state before and after each choice.
- Locate published notices, rights requests, preference withdrawal, deletion, export, incident, processor, and retention controls. Missing runtime access is unverified evidence.
- Separate a technical observation from the legal question it may raise. Record the source, date, jurisdiction, owner, smallest remedy, and verification needed.

Do not infer a universal consent pattern, response deadline, disclosure, or release blocker from a checklist entry.
