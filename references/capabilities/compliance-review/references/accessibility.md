# Accessibility evidence prompts

Use after identifying the product journey, contractual standard, users, and current applicable sources.

- Select material journeys and inspect semantic structure, names, states, focus order, keyboard operation, errors, contrast, zoom/reflow, motion, media, and authentication.
- Use automated checks as leads. Confirm material findings through the relevant manual journey and assistive technology when available.
- Record the exact journey, device/browser, observation, affected user outcome, standard question, owner, smallest repair, and repeatable verification.
- Report unavailable devices, assistive technologies, or live journeys as coverage gaps. Do not infer conformance from a score or one tool.

Contractual and legal standards are inputs discovered for the target; this reference does not establish them.
