# Consumer evidence prompts

Use after identifying the offered product, markets, pricing, subscription behavior, communications, marketplace role, and current authoritative sources.

- Trace an authorized synthetic journey from offer through payment, confirmation, cancellation, refund, account deletion, and support. Never submit a real order or message.
- Record displayed price components, terms, recurring charges, cancellation controls, communications consent, ranking/review behavior, and content-reporting controls where relevant.
- Compare observed behavior with the current requirement question for the identified market. Keep commercial policy, legal interpretation, and technical evidence distinct.
- Return an evidence ledger with owner, smallest remedy, verification, and any missing product or jurisdiction input.

An incomplete journey is unverified, not a generic launch blocker.
