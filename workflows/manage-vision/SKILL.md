# Manage Vision

Maintain the project’s vision anchor; it does not own roadmap, implementation, or decision logs. Locate, audit, and sync are read-only.

## Workflow

1. Find the established anchor from consumer documentation; use a default only when no usable convention exists.
2. For authorized create/update, write supported goals, audience, constraints, non-goals, outcome, sources, history, and decision test to the established anchor; preserve existing history and mark unresolved strategy provisional.
3. For audit/sync, compare proposal, roadmap, or shipped evidence against the anchor and report alignment, conflict, or unknown direction.
4. Recommend the smallest direction decision or next outcome, with its consequence. Ask only when strategy or authority is genuinely missing.
5. Verify links, history, and downstream contradictions.
6. Use independent proposal/challenge or review/critique only for distinct material questions. Recheck after changed evidence or a repair. If blocked, return the cause, options, recommendation, consequence, and next action.

## Definition of Done

- Anchor location, operation, and sources are explicit.
- Anchor includes supported direction and decision test, or reports missing elements with a recommendation.
- Read-only work returns findings without edits.
- Authorized edits preserve history and uncertainty.
