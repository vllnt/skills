# AI and content evidence prompts

Use after identifying automation, model/provider roles, user-facing decisions, generated media, training-data claims, UGC, moderation, and target markets.

- Map what the product represents to users, who can act on generated output, what recourse exists, and which evidence supports those claims.
- Inspect product disclosures, generated-content provenance, user reporting, moderation, appeal, and policy controls only where the feature is in scope.
- Separate provider claims from the product's observed behavior. Retrieve current official requirements before classifying a legal obligation.
- Record feature, market question, evidence, affected user outcome, owner, smallest technical or policy question, and verification.

Do not treat an AI feature, watermark, human handoff, or content workflow as universally required.
