## Goal

Maintain an evidence-backed roadmap and, when asked, the project vision anchor and decision test that guide it, preserving direction and history. The roadmap owns sequencing and the anchor owns direction; neither executes work, replaces an issue tracker, or owns the decision log.

### Definition of Done

- Operation (roadmap or vision anchor), authoritative location, mode, sources, and criteria are explicit.
- Maintained work preserves traceability, history, and a current recommended next outcome or decision; the anchor has supported direction and a decision test, or missing elements have a recommendation.
- Read-only output distinguishes shipped, planned, blocked, and unverified work and reports anchor alignment, conflict, or unknown direction, without edits.
- Authorized edits have verified transitions, links, history, and downstream contradictions.
- Current validation accepts the selected mode; no required finding remains.

## Workflow

1. Resolve operation, product evidence, current work, delivery evidence, applicable instructions, and validation criteria. Find the established roadmap or anchor from consumer documentation and instructions; use a default ([roadmap format](references/format.md), [vision anchor format](references/vision-format.md)) only when no usable convention exists.
2. Keep outcomes, work, evidence, dependencies, decisions, and uncertainty traceable to their owner; propose parallel lanes only per [parallelism](references/parallel.md). For an authorized anchor create or update, prepare supported goals, audience, constraints, non-goals, outcome, sources, history, and decision test, preserving existing history and marking unresolved strategy provisional.
3. For anchor audit or sync, compare proposals, roadmap, or shipped evidence against the anchor and report alignment, conflict, or unknown direction.
4. Recommend the next outcome or the smallest direction decision and its consequence; make reversible sequencing choices from evidence.
5. For substantive work, use [Quality validation](references/vstack/protocols/quality-validation.md) before authorized maintenance.
6. In maintenance mode, apply only the validated edit, preserving completed and dropped history and human decisions. Verify links, status transitions, history, downstream contradictions, and applicable checks.
