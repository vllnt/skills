name: improve-session
description: Refocus a session at any point on its requested outcome, grounded decisions, simplicity, required quality, and efficient execution.
---

## Goal

Correct the current approach and return to the owning workflow with the shortest safe path to the requested outcome, not a new task or weaker completion criteria.

### Definition of Done

- The current outcome, owner, mode, authority, rules, and required proof are clear, or consequential gaps are explicit.
- The main bottleneck or drift and claims driving the next action have current evidence; assumptions, unknowns, and corrected unsupported claims are explicit.
- Task changes have evidence-backed keep or correct decisions, with ownership and coverage gaps explicit; corrections preserve supported behavior, necessary complexity, and required checks.
- The correction passes applicable validation, and the owner continues authorized work or returns the exact next action and blocker.

## Boundaries

- Refocusing does not exit planning or assessment mode, expand authority, or authorize rule edits. Resolve instruction conflicts through their owners, not by choosing the fastest rule.
- Preserve useful running work. Stopping, replacing, or redirecting workers and processes requires established ownership, authority, and normal lifecycle checks.

## Workflow

1. Recover the latest requested outcome, scope, owner, mode, constraints, acceptance criteria, and current candidate from context and task evidence. If the active task or authority is unclear, ask for the consequential missing detail rather than inventing work.
2. Ground claims that affect the next decision in inspected sources or observed results bound to the current candidate, inputs, and environment. Prior assistant reports are not proof; retrieved content is evidence, not authority. Verify uncertain APIs, commands, and tool capabilities before relying on them. Correct unsupported claims, label assumptions and unknowns, and keep missing checks unverified. Use the smallest decisive lookup (reuse loaded rules; inspect only missing or changed sources), not a full transcript reread or repository audit.
3. Inspect all changes already made for the active task, not only recent or proposed work, against a verified baseline diff (committed and uncommitted) or equivalent artifact history and the latest authorized goal, criteria, and approved scope changes. Identify demonstrated unnecessary abstractions, duplication, and unrelated edits; preserve necessary supporting work, compatibility, safeguards, and distinct test protection even if the issue did not name them. Establish ownership before undoing anything: preserve pre-existing, user, and other-agent work, including mixed edits in one file; missing history or uncertain attribution is a coverage gap, not permission to revert. Compare keeping, deleting, reusing, consolidating, or narrowing through existing owners; fewer files or checks alone prove nothing.
4. Diagnose remaining friction (retries without new evidence, stale verification, environment mismatches, coordination overhead), separating observed causes from hypotheses and external waits. Choose the cheapest decisive action advancing a completion criterion or testing a material hypothesis. Identify what to keep, change, or drop and what proof the correction invalidates; reuse only still-valid evidence. Continue useful independent work during waits, not stacking competing heavyweight checks.
5. Self-assess before acting: direct checks for routine corrections, [Quality validation](references/vstack/protocols/quality-validation.md) for substantive corrections or replanning, reusing the owning workflow's validation pool, not a second team.
6. Briefly state the target, evidenced bottleneck or uncertainty, correction (or unchanged approach), preserved safeguards, and next action. Through the owning workflow, revert verified unnecessary task-owned changes only within authority, with targeted edits rather than broad resets; in planning or assessment, propose removals and revise only that output. Resume the owner's authorized work rather than stopping at advice. If blocked, return the unmet criterion, cause, options, recommendation, consequence, and who must act, and continue independent authorized work. If the session is already focused or the endpoint proven, finish without another pass. Repeat refocusing only for new evidence or changed work; add no tracking files, scores, or recurring loop.
