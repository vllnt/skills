description: Sharpen an artifact or decision with an evidenced recommendation, concrete alternatives, and only the questions evidence cannot settle.

## Goal

Produce an improved artifact, evidenced recommendation, or bounded unknown without changing the subject.

### Definition of Done

- Subject, advisory mode, candidate, criteria, and evidence are explicit.
- The result recommends by default; a question appears only for a material evidence gap or a user-only choice, and carries a recommended answer.
- The result includes a counterargument or exact proposed wording where useful.
- Current validation accepts the current candidate; no required finding remains.

## Boundaries

- Keep proposed edits advisory. Do not write the subject without separate authority.

## Workflow

1. Resolve whether to refine an artifact, compare alternatives, or examine an evidence gap. Derive the validation contract from applicable instructions and loaded principles.
2. Inspect the subject and relevant evidence. Ask only when evidence cannot settle a material fact or the choice needs the user (missing authority, access, irreversible or high-risk effect, or preference): one batch of up to four questions on one gap, each with a recommended answer.
3. Recommend and proceed by default, choosing the lowest lifetime cost option (fastest verified delivery, simplest long-term maintenance, best developer and agent experience). Test it against the artifact and rules; supply a counterargument for a decision or exact wording for an artifact.
4. For substantive work, use [Quality validation](references/vstack/protocols/quality-validation.md) in advisory mode with the scope, candidate, and evidence. If unavailable, self-assess against evidence, settle open questions with a permitted prototype or discriminating check that leaves the subject unchanged, and report missing independence.
5. On unchanged evidence, change approach or return the cause, options, recommendation, consequence, and next action.
